Case studies

Strategy · Retail · Self-initiated

How could Primark grow again without touching price?

The brief
Working on Primark's shop floor during our MBA, a classmate and I saw sales being lost that never showed up in a report, while like-for-like growth had stalled and margin was guided down to about 10%.
What we did
Combined what we saw on the floor with public data to map a ten-year threat horizon (regulation, rising UK store costs, Shein, AI shopping agents), then built a five-pillar, margin-safe blueprint, from staffing to footfall to a loyalty layer in the app, costed from a 15-store pilot to full rollout, with control-group pilots and a risk register.
Result
A costed plan sizing a 2–3% like-for-like uplift, about £190–285m of extra annual sales (illustrative), for £45–75m of mostly low-capital pilots, written for discussion with Primark's leadership.
Strategy · Market entry

Should a Spanish delivery-management business enter the UK?

The brief
theRest, a restaurant delivery-management business in Spain, wanted a plan for entering the UK and Ireland.
What I did
With an MBA consulting team, researched UK delivery platforms and restaurants over 3–4 months, rebuilt a five-year unit-economics model on the client's real operating data and designed a London-first go-to-market and sales funnel.
Result
A 15.5x LTV/CAC case, presented to the leadership team in Barcelona, behind their capital-light “go” decision.

Founder · P&L and people

What I did
Grew it to five brands plus an online supermarket from one site. Hired a team of 12 and a marketing intern, planned shifts around order volumes, set prices from labour and overhead analysis, cut operating costs 20%, and reported the numbers and the next three months' plan to investors.
Result

Data to decisions · Staffing

Turning call data into staffing decisions

The brief
Many of Exotel's customers ran support and collections teams that had to match agents to call demand.
What I did
Analysed customers' call volumes to show when calls peaked and when to schedule agents, and built ROI cases from their own numbers: cost saved, time saved and customers served.
Result
ROI cases the economic buyer validated, and an analysis I went on to run on deal after deal.
